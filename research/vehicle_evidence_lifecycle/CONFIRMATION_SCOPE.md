# Confirmation scope

Fresh six seeds, three prescribed profiles and new closed-loop trajectories are frozen before confirmation scoring. The public medium/hard recordings were already consumed during earlier development; this extension does not claim unseen-public-recording generalization. They remain transparent stress proxies with all tails retained. No protected evaluation payload is opened.

The1080trials include all twelve conditions, including the three predeclared assumption-breaking controls. Report all outcomes. Joint conditional safety is evaluated only under explicit premises; overall empirical outcomes are separate. Native published-framework superiority, first-ever novelty, human effectiveness, physical hardware validation and journal acceptance are not certified by this simulation.

No confirmation result may trigger method or threshold tuning. A scientific failure is retained in its report. A software verification failure pauses final sealing; any repair must preserve the original sources/results and use versioned evidence. Every completed trial is reused by identity/hash on resume; no completed predecessor experiment is repeated.
